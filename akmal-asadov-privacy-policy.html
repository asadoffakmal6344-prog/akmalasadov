Privacy Policy — Akmal Asadov MCP Connector

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Akmal Asadov MCP Connector ("the app") is a private, single-user tool. It connects the owner's own Pinterest business account to an AI assistant through the Pinterest MCP server, using the Pinterest API.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner, Akmal Asadov. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other Pinterest user's account.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's authorization, the app may access:

  • the owner's Pinterest account profile;
  • the owner's own boards and Pins;
  • analytics for the owner's own account and Pins;
  • Pins the owner chooses to create or schedule.

How the data is used

Data is used only to let the owner view, analyse and publish content on their own Pinterest account. The app does not sell, rent or share Pinterest data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising profiling.

Storage and security

The Pinterest access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Data retrieved from Pinterest is not stored on any server operated by the app. The owner can revoke access at any time from Pinterest settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
